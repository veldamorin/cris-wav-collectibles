Ralli Cris Queda

Passionate music lover and collector, founder of CrisWav Collectibles

Music has been an essential part of Ralli Cris Queda's life since childhood. Over the years, personal listening habits shifted across various genres, but K-pop eventually stuck out as the ultimate favorite, complemented by a deep appreciation for Hip-Hop and R&B.

CrisWav Collectibles was founded to bridge these soundscapes into one physical space. The store was built to offer collectors and fans authentic K-pop merchandise, rare photocards, and official items alongside albums and collectibles celebrating Hip-Hop and R&B culture.
